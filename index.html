About Godey

Godey is a beautiful town in the Somali Region of Ethiopia, known for the Shabelle River, fertile farmland, and the rich culture and traditions of its people. The town is part of the wider Ogaden region, which is home to significant natural gas resources and has considerable oil and hydrocarbon potential.
